### Section 1
*** Data Models ***
- Product: Product Model representa individual items available in the store. With attributes id, name, description, price, image_url, category and also connected by reference to the order_items 

- Order : This model manages orders with attributes: order_id, customer_id, total_price, status, created_at and connected by reference to order_items

- OrderItem : This represents the items within an order with attributes. order_item_id, 
order_id, 
product_id, 
quantity, 
price.
